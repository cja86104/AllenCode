Evergreen: proposed stock photos

Each of the 14 photo slots, shown at the exact crop and shape the template uses. All are free Unsplash photos (no Unsplash+), hotlinked the same way as Copper Thistle. Where no free photo matched the description exactly, the alt text is rewritten to describe what the photo really shows, and the caption changes only where it would otherwise contradict the picture (5 of 14).
